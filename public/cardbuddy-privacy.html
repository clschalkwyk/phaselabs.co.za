CardBuddy · Android

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

CardBuddy is a digital business-card app from PhaseLabs. This policy explains how the Android app handles your card information and how the separate beta signup form handles your tester email. For privacy questions, email phaselabs.za@gmail.com.

Information you add to CardBuddy

You can enter card names, your name, title, organisation, pronouns, tagline, email address, phone number and links. You can also choose a profile photo or background artwork using Android’s image picker. CardBuddy saves card details and settings in the app’s private storage and copies chosen images into private app files.

CardBuddy uses this information to show and edit your cards, generate contact or link QR codes on your device, and prepare image or PDF exports. The app does not require a CardBuddy account or sync cards to a PhaseLabs server. The current app has no configured advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Sharing and other apps

When you show a contact QR code, someone who scans it can receive the contact details and links you entered. A link QR code contains the selected link. People who view your card can also copy or photograph what you display.

When you choose Share image or Share PDF, CardBuddy creates a file in its cache and gives temporary access to the app or recipient you select through Android’s share menu. That app or recipient may save or send the copy under its own practices. CardBuddy does not send your cards to a PhaseLabs server in the current app.

Beta signup and support email

The optional beta signup form linked from the CardBuddy website is hosted by Google Forms, outside the app. It asks for the Google Play account email you submit. PhaseLabs uses that email to add you to the Google Play tester list and communicate about testing. Google processes the form under its own terms and privacy policy.

If you email us for support or privacy questions, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include through our email service. To ask about removal of a beta signup, tester-list entry or support email, contact phaselabs.za@gmail.com.

Storage, backup and deletion

CardBuddy stores card details and imported images in Android’s app-private storage. Android backup is enabled. Depending on your device, account and backup settings, Android or Google may back up or transfer app data. PhaseLabs does not control copies held by those services; backup and restore behaviour can vary.

You can edit or clear card fields in the app. Removing a photo or artwork from a card, or resetting its styling, does not necessarily delete the image file already copied into app storage. Resetting styling keeps contact details and the profile photo.

To remove the app’s current local data and files, clear CardBuddy’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. This does not erase copies already shared with other people or apps, files saved elsewhere, or operating-system backups. Manage those copies with the relevant recipient or service.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if CardBuddy’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. For questions about this policy, email phaselabs.za@gmail.com.
